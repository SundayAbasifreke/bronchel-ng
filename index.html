Your Advert Is Getting Attention. But Is It Bringing You Customers?

Imagine spending money on adverts, then spending the next few days answering messages from people who aren't ready to buy.

Someone asks for your price. Another asks what you offer. Someone else asks where you are. You answer everyone and follow up.

Then they disappear.

It isn't always because people aren't interested. Often, your advertising process attracts enquiries without checking if those people really want to buy.

Common reasons your adverts don't convert:

1. Everyone goes straight to WhatsApp or Instagram messages.

They click and open a chat before they understand your offer, your prices or why they should choose you. You end up answering questions that should have been answered before they contacted you.

2. Your advertising has no clear offer.

Saying what your business does is not enough. People need to know what you're offering, why it matters to them and why you're worth considering.

3. Your campaign isn't properly structured.

Running an advert is not the same as having an advertising strategy. Without the right objective, targeting, message and customer journey, you pay for the wrong kind of attention.

4. You attract enquiries, not qualified prospects.

Some people are just browsing. Others are comparing. Some only want to ask questions. Your process should separate casual interest from real buying interest.
